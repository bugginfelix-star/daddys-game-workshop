Learning about eeveelousons

Meet, learn, trade, battle, and protect the Evo Pals!

Adella's colorful pet adventure. Learn about 9 original Evo Pals, unlock helpers, collect rewards, and defeat the silly Grumble Collector.

Original copyright-safe Evo Pals. No online trading. Progress saves on this device.

How to Play

Learn: Pick a stage and read about an Evo Pal.

Quiz: Answer a simple question to earn Learning Stars.

Protect: Battle friendly trouble creatures using Attack, Special, Shield, and Heal.

Unlock Helpers: Complete stages to unlock pets. Pick one active helper in the Pet Book.

Trade: Trade earned hearts and gems with friendly NPCs. No online trading.

Boss: Protect all Evo Pals from The Grumble Collector after all 9 stages are complete.
